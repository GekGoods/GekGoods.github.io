How it works

The app connects your phone, your adapter, and your mobile provider.

Plug in your adapter

Insert the adapter like a SIM card, or connect a supported card reader.

Add your mobile plan

Use the activation code or QR code from your mobile provider. You need an internet connection for this step.

Choose your active plan

See the plans on the adapter and switch, turn off, or remove them when your adapter supports it.

Check your setup before you install.

The Google Play version works on Android 9 and newer. It is made for removable adapters and supported card readers, rather than the eSIM built into the phone.
