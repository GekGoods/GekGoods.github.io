Designed around the hardware you own.

GekGoods SIM Manager is an independent build for compatible removable eSIM hardware. It keeps the important parts visible: the adapter, the profile provider, and the result.

01

Manage profiles

Use the app to download, enable, disable, and remove profiles supported by your adapter and eSIM provider.

02

Use a removable adapter

Connect a supported removable eSIM adapter, or use a compatible USB CCID reader when your setup supports it.

03

Know what to expect

Clear compatibility notes and practical support guidance help you decide whether your Android device is a good host.

How it works

The app is a control surface for your adapter and the eSIM service you choose. It does not replace your mobile operator.

Connect compatible hardware

Insert the removable adapter or connect a supported USB CCID reader to your Android device.

Start an eSIM action

Use the activation details from your eSIM provider. The app connects to the provider’s SM-DP+ service over HTTPS.

Manage the result

Review the available profiles and use the adapter controls your hardware supports.

Check your setup before you install.

The unprivileged Play build targets Android 9 and newer. Internal phone eSIM access is not promised by this build; removable adapters and supported USB readers are the intended paths.
