Get started

Download the app to check.

The quickest way to check your setup is to install the app, connect your adapter, and follow the prompts. This page gives you the simple details before you begin.

Download and checkGet support

Start here: download GekGoods SIM Manager, connect your removable adapter or card reader, and let the app check whether they work together. The Google Play version works on Android 9 and newer.

Android phone

Use Android 9 or newer. Different phone models can behave differently, even when they run the same Android version.

Removable adapter

Use an adapter supported by the app. Some adapter makers need to approve an app before it can use the adapter, so check that GekGoods SIM Manager is supported.

USB card reader

A compatible card reader can let your phone manage the adapter. You may need an OTG cable or a powered hub.

Internet

You need internet when adding a plan. Your mobile provider’s service handles the download.

Samsung phones

If you use a Samsung phone, turn on the Samsung option in the app settings when the app tells you to. There is only one GekGoods download.

Phone’s built-in eSIM

This version is for removable adapters and card readers. It does not promise access to the eSIM built into your phone.

What to do first

  1. Download GekGoods SIM Manager from Google Play and open it on the Android phone you plan to use.
  2. Connect your removable adapter or USB card reader and follow the app’s instructions.
  3. If the app says the adapter is not supported, ask the adapter maker about GekGoods SIM Manager.
  4. Keep your eSIM provider’s activation code private and use only providers you trust.

The app’s result is the best first guide for your exact phone and adapter. Android, adapter, or provider updates can change compatibility.
