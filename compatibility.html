Before you install

Check your setup first.

The app can only manage hardware and profile services that your device, adapter, and signing configuration allow. Use this checklist to avoid surprises.

Quick answer: the unprivileged Play build supports Android 9 and newer, compatible removable eSIM hardware, and supported USB CCID readers. A phone’s built-in eSIM is not the target of this build.

Android host

Use Android 9 (API 28) or newer. Compatibility still depends on the phone’s USB, SIM, and secure-element implementation.

Removable eSIM adapter

Your adapter must expose the interface expected by the app. For native removable-eSIM access, the adapter vendor may need to allowlist this app’s signing certificate.

USB CCID reader

A reader path requires Android USB host support, a suitable OTG connection, and a standard T=0 CCID reader. Some phones need a powered hub.

Network access

Profile downloads use the SM-DP+ service selected by your eSIM provider. Allow internet access and follow that provider’s activation instructions.

Samsung hosts

Use the Samsung compatibility option in the app settings when it applies to your device. The GekGoods listing uses one app; no separate Samsung download is required.

Internal phone eSIM

The unprivileged Play build does not promise management of the eSIM built into a phone. Use a supported removable adapter or USB reader instead.

Before you rely on it

  1. Test the exact Play-signed build on the Android device and adapter you plan to use.
  2. Confirm with your adapter vendor that the GekGoods signing certificate is allowlisted where native access requires it.
  3. Keep your eSIM provider’s activation code private and use only providers you trust.
  4. If a setup fails, record the Android version, device model, adapter or reader model, and the exact error for support.

Hardware support can change with Android updates, adapter firmware, and eSIM provider policies. A compatibility check is guidance, not a guarantee.
