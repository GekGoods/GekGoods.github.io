Before you install

Check your setup first.

The app works when your phone, adapter, and mobile provider can work together. This short checklist will help you know what to expect.

Quick answer: the Google Play version works on Android 9 and newer, with compatible removable adapters and card readers. It is not designed to manage the eSIM already built into your phone.

Android phone

Use Android 9 or newer. Different phone models can behave differently, even when they run the same Android version.

Removable adapter

Use an adapter supported by the app. Some adapter makers need to approve an app before it can use the adapter, so check that GekGoods SIM Manager is supported.

USB card reader

A compatible card reader can let your phone manage the adapter. You may need an OTG cable or a powered hub.

Internet

You need internet when adding a plan. Your mobile provider’s service handles the download.

Samsung phones

If you use a Samsung phone, turn on the Samsung option in the app settings when the app tells you to. There is only one GekGoods download.

Phone’s built-in eSIM

This version is for removable adapters and card readers. It does not promise access to the eSIM built into your phone.

Before you get started

  1. Install the Google Play version on the phone and adapter you plan to use.
  2. Ask the adapter maker whether GekGoods SIM Manager is supported.
  3. Keep your eSIM provider’s activation code private and use only providers you trust.
  4. If something fails, note your phone model, Android version, adapter or reader, and the message you saw.

Updates to Android, adapter software, or your provider can change compatibility. The checklist is a guide, not a guarantee.
