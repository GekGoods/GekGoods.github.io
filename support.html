01

Start with the basics

Make sure your phone is running Android 9 or newer, reconnect the adapter, allow USB access if asked, and check that you have internet.

02

Try the right path

If your phone’s built-in eSIM does not work with the app, use a supported removable adapter or card reader.

03

Share useful details

Tell us your phone model, Android version, adapter or reader, app version, and the message you saw.

Protect your mobile plan details

Never post an activation code, QR code, phone or SIM identifiers, or a complete diagnostic log. These details can identify or give access to your mobile plan. Hide them before sharing, for example with [redacted].

What to include in a useful report

  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Android version and phone model.
  • Adapter or USB reader model, including firmware if known.
  • Whether you were using a Samsung phone or a card reader.
  • The smallest set of steps that reproduces the problem.

Community questions

GitHub Discussions is planned as the community space. Until it is enabled, use the repository issue tracker for support. Please do not include activation codes or other private plan details.
