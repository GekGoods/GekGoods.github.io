Privacy policy

Privacy should be easy to understand.

This policy explains what the GekGoods SIM Manager Android app processes when you use it with compatible eSIM hardware.

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Short version: the app is a local Android client for your eSIM hardware and provider. It does not require a GekGoods account. When you download or manage an eSIM profile, the provider’s SM-DP+ service processes the information required for that operation.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the GekGoods SIM Manager Android application, package name com.gekgoods.simmanager, published by GekGoods. It does not cover your mobile operator, eSIM provider, adapter manufacturer, Google Play, or GitHub. Those services have their own policies.

Information the app processes

To operate, the app can process information needed to communicate with your connected removable eSIM hardware or supported USB CCID reader. Depending on the action, this can include profile names, eSIM identifiers, device capability information, activation details, and commands exchanged with the hardware.

When you start a profile download or another provider operation, the app connects to the SM-DP+ service specified by your eSIM provider. The connection uses HTTPS where the service supports the required protocol. The provider controls its own processing, retention, and privacy practices.

What GekGoods receives

The current app is designed to work without a GekGoods account or a GekGoods-operated profile backend. GekGoods does not receive activation codes or eSIM profiles through a GekGoods server as part of the normal app flow. Your eSIM provider may still receive information directly from the app when you use that provider’s service.

Diagnostics and support

The app and Android system may produce technical logs. If verbose diagnostics are enabled, logs can contain request or response details, endpoint information, or eSIM-related identifiers. You choose whether to share a log with support. Redact activation codes, QR codes, EIDs, ICCIDs, and profile identifiers before posting to GitHub Discussions.

Local storage, retention, and deletion

Profile state and app settings may remain on your device so the app can display and manage your hardware. You can remove local app data through Android Settings, remove profiles through the app where supported, or uninstall the app. Data held by your eSIM provider must be deleted or retained through that provider.

Sharing and advertising

The current GekGoods build is intended to operate without advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information. If a future release introduces a materially different data practice, this policy will be updated before that release.

Security

Network requests to provider services use HTTPS when supported by the service. No software or network connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep activation details private and install the app only from a source you trust.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will change with a new version.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the GekGoods site repository. GitHub Discussions will become the community support space when it is enabled. Do not include activation codes or other eSIM secrets in a public post.
