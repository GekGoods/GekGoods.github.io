Privacy policy

Privacy should be easy to understand.

This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it with a removable eSIM adapter or card reader.

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Short version: the app runs on your Android phone and talks to your adapter and mobile provider. It does not require a GekGoods account. When you add or manage a mobile plan, the provider’s download service processes the information needed for that action.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers GekGoods SIM Manager, the Android app published by GekGoods (Google Play package com.gekgoods.simmanager). It does not cover your mobile operator, eSIM provider, adapter manufacturer, Google Play, or GitHub. Those services have their own policies.

Information used to run the app

The app uses information needed to talk to your removable adapter or card reader. Depending on what you do, this can include mobile-plan names, eSIM identifiers such as an EID or ICCID, phone compatibility details such as the phone model and (when your provider requires it) the IMEI, activation details, and commands sent to the hardware.

When you add a plan, the app connects to the download service named by your mobile provider. Depending on the provider and activation flow, it may send the provider an activation code, matching ID, confirmation code, and the phone's IMEI. This connection uses HTTPS. Your provider controls what it does with that information and how long it keeps it.

What GekGoods sees

The current app does not need a GekGoods account or a GekGoods server to add a plan. GekGoods does not receive your activation codes, IMEI, or mobile-plan profiles through a GekGoods server during normal use. Your mobile provider may still receive information directly from the app when you use its service.

Troubleshooting logs

The app and Android may create technical logs. If detailed logging is turned on, a log can include connection details, your phone model or Android version, an EID, ICCID, IMEI, or other eSIM information. You decide whether to share a log with support. Hide activation codes, QR codes, phone or SIM identifiers, and plan numbers before posting.

Information kept on your phone

The app may keep plan status and settings on your phone so it can show and manage your adapter. Clear the app’s data in Android Settings, remove plans in the app where supported, or uninstall the app. Your mobile provider controls information on its own systems.

Advertising and sharing

The current build is intended to work without advertising or analytics software. We do not sell personal information. If a later version changes what it does with information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Keeping information safe

The app uses HTTPS for its provider connections. No software or network connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep activation details private and install the app only from a source you trust.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will change with a new version.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the GekGoods site repository. GitHub Discussions will become the community support space when it is enabled. Do not include activation codes or other eSIM secrets in a public post.
